- [DONE in v2] Best model selection was tracking lowest val ELBO from epoch 1, including
  during KL annealing when beta=0. Fixed in v2: only start tracking best model after

- Temporal regularisation: the baseline VAE encodes each day independently — consecutive
  days for the same patient show no coherent trajectory in latent space (confirmed by
  latent_trajectories.png). Fix: add a temporal smoothness penalty to the loss:
    loss = recon + β·KL + γ·||z(t) - z(t-1)||²
  This requires the DataLoader to yield consecutive-day pairs rather than shuffled
  individual days. Weight γ controls the smoothness/reconstruction tradeoff. This is a
  prerequisite for RQ1 (drift detection) to be meaningful.
